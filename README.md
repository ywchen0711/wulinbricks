# 武林積木 Wulin Bricks

The people of the martial world as brick minifigures, one at a time, each with their moves. Each one stands on a
practice ground at dusk, where you can watch their moves performed in 3D.

**Live:** https://ywchen0711.github.io/wulinbricks/ · **《龍門雙奕》第一集 龍門血案:** https://ywchen0711.github.io/wulinbricks/film.html · **番外篇 一字千金:** https://ywchen0711.github.io/wulinbricks/film.html?ep=show1 · the first test film: film.html?ep=proto · the season plan: [docs/season1.html](docs/season1.html)

The page has three modes. **Practice** shows one character on the practice ground, trying moves on a post and a
wall. **Duel** puts the brothers face to face to spar, with buttons to try a light hit, a heavy hit or a knockdown
on either, and an auto-sparring loop. **Story** plays the films.

## 奕叡 Yi Rui · 啟程刀客 The Departing Blade

A young swordsman who left home with an old saber. He wears an indigo robe crossed over a white one, a blue
headband with flying ties, a high tail through a silver ring, a scabbard at the hip, and a broad saber with a ring
pommel.

**一刀啟程 One Cut to Set Out.** He gathers his qi: the blade lights up and the wind turns round his feet. He lifts
the saber over his head, steps in and cuts down once. The cut leaves the blade as an upright crescent of sword qi.
It runs along the ground, splits the training post and cuts a slit through the stone wall behind it, leaving a
glowing cut in the floor.

## 奕碩 Yi Shuo · 旋風少俠 The Whirlwind Kid

Yi Rui's younger brother, a minifig on short legs. He wears a vermilion jacket tied with his brother's indigo sash, a
red headband and white wraps on his trousers. He never cared for blades: his weapon is a football. At rest he keeps
it up off his foot.

**旋風球 Whirlwind Ball.** He flicks the ball up, and the wind winds round it into a little whirlwind while it spins
faster and faster. He turns right round, leg far back, and kicks it with everything. The ball goes off in a drill
of wind and tears through the post, whose bricks are carried up and away. It punches a round hole in the wall, then
flies back in a high arc, and he traps it under his foot.

**Controls:** the move button, Space or 1 performs it, and Tab or the tabs at the top switch the character. Drag to look around, use the wheel or pinch to zoom, and
double-click (or C) to get the auto camera back. S toggles slow motion, R resets the targets.

## 冷笑天 Leng Xiaotian · 暴龍 The Tyrant Dragon

A cold master. He wears a black robe printed with gold dragon scales over crimson, a crimson cape, long silver hair
and a gold band with two dragon horns; his eyes are shadowed and one corner of his mouth sneers. He fights bare-handed
with his palms. At rest he stands with his hands behind his back and his chin up.

**笑盡天下 Laugh Down the World.** Three cold laughs, echoing. He draws his palms back to his hips while crimson qi
gathers in them, then drives both forward with a shout. A great glowing palm print goes out, crimson and gold, with
a cone of roaring wind behind it. It is wide and does not cut: it sweeps away the post and a whole stretch of the wall,
and throws down anyone in front of it. Its power runs a little higher than the brothers' moves, so in a clash it
usually wins.

In the duel, either side can be any of the three.

## 黑風寨 The Black Wind gang

They are the villains of episode 1. 黑熊 Black Bear is the bearded chief with a wooden club. 刀疤 Scar is the second,
with a scar across his eye and a broadsword. 瘦猴 Skinny Monkey is the quick lackey. Their moves are 重拳 Heavy
Punch and 掄棍 Club Swing.

## 《龍門雙奕》第一集 龍門血案 Episode 1: Blood at Longmen (about 7½ minutes, voiced)

- **Cold open.** In the Listening-to-Rain teahouse, Master Bai the storyteller tells the town that the Alliance chief
  died last night at the Longmen Manor. He tells it before anyone else knows.
- **The day before, at the manor.** The father teaches Yi Rui the saber, and Yi Shuo smashes a lantern with his ball.
  The brothers spar, blade against ball, and cancel out. The father mends the ball: "this ball matters more than
  your life." The chief arrives with Tie Wusi of the Law Hall.
- **That night.** A shadow crosses the wall, then a scream: the chief lies dead with a Longmen cut. The father is
  seized: "Take your brother and go."
- **At dawn on the road.** The bandits come for the ball (the test film below) and Yi Rui saves his brother. A Law
  Hall token is dropped, and the split ball holds a jade carved with a green dragon. Three cold laughs come from the
  hill.

New for it:

- the Longmen Manor and teahouse sets, and day, dusk, night and dawn light;
- the father, Tie Wusi, the chief, Master Bai, guards and listeners;
- entrance cards with each character's four-line poem;
- move callouts and slow-motion strikes;
- recorded voices with mouths that follow them;
- props;
- a camera that lifts bricks or people out of its way.

## 《龍門雙奕》番外篇 一字千金 A Word Worth Gold (film.html?ep=show1, about 10 minutes, voiced)

The brothers on a children's quiz show of Chinese characters, hosted by 曾國城, in a television studio of bricks
(js/studio.js): a great screen under the show's name, six podiums with name plates, lit scores and buzzers, the host's
desk, coin towers, a truss of lamps with sweeping beams, an audience of bricks people on four rows who laugh, clap,
cheer and go "aww" (real audience recordings, all CC0, cut and layered by tools/crowd_real.py into audio/sfx; the sources are listed there), and confetti and gold
coins at the end. Six children (js/cast/show.js): 陳小雷 who buzzes before the question, 林書涵 who reads the dictionary
for fun, 奕叡 with his saber and his "在下", 奕碩 with his ball (the host confiscates it), 王大寶 whose head is full of
food, and 張圓圓 who whispers and is never wrong.

- Round one, readings: 龜裂, 莘莘學子, 一曝十寒.
- Round two, the wrong character: 再接再厲, 迫不及待, 一鼓作氣, 走投無路.
- Round three, the missing character: 畫蛇添足, 對牛彈琴, 狼吞虎嚥, 先發制人, 一箭雙鵰.
- The final, 一字千金: 龘, then 龜, both right every time, until the last question (one character for the person who
  matters most): the elder writes 弟, the younger 哥, and the host can't judge it. A draw.
- Then the host gives the ball back, and it goes straight into the screen.

The film is written as it runs (each scene a run of lines, pauses and cues, its moments counted from the recorded
lengths), so lines can be re-recorded without retiming anything. A line may carry what the voice should read when it
differs from the subtitle (bopomofo, a misreading on purpose, 曾 as a surname).

## The test film 奕碩遇險 Yi Shuo in Trouble (film.html?ep=proto)

At dusk, outside the village, Yi Shuo is playing ball when the Black Wind come and want it. His Whirlwind Ball
knocks Skinny Monkey flat, but Scar's fist and Black Bear's club put him on the ground. As the club goes up again,
Yi Rui arrives, and his One Cut to Set Out knocks all three down; they run. The episode has subtitles in Chinese
and English (no recorded voices yet). Drag the picture to look around while it plays.

## Taking a blow

`js/react.js` decides how a figure takes a hit:

- **light:** a flinch, eyes shut, a half step back;
- **heavy:** thrown back, arms flung out, staggering, then walking back to his mark;
- **down:** thrown through the air, flat on his back and dazed, then up in one spring (鯉魚打挺) and back to his mark.

A film can hold someone down until a cue releases him.

## When things meet

`js/physics.js` handles collisions.

- **Blows meeting blows.** A crescent of sword qi, a whirlwind ball and other projectiles are things in the world.
  When two from different fighters meet, they clash in a burst of light.
  - If their power is close (each strike's power varies a little), they cancel each other out. The ball is not
    destroyed: it is knocked into the air and goes on under its own physics.
  - Otherwise the stronger breaks through, weakened by what it overcame, and strikes with less force.
- **Bodies.** Figures cannot pass through each other. Standing figures shove apart, and one thrown back by a blow
  crashes into whoever is behind him and knocks him on.
- **The loose ball.** It bounces, rolls to a stop and glances off anyone it hits; Yi Shuo goes to fetch it and
  dribbles it home.

In the duel, **⚔ 同時出招 Both at once** has the brothers strike together, and auto sparring does this every third
round.

## Making a video (for YouTube)

**From the page.** Press **⬇ 下載影片 Download video** on the episode's control bar. The episode plays through once
from the start while the picture, the words over it and the sound are recorded. When it ends, a video file is saved:
MP4 in Chrome and Edge, WebM elsewhere. YouTube takes either. It records in real time, so a slow machine gives a
choppier video.

**Best quality.** Render it frame by frame:

```
npm install
node tools/render.mjs ep1               # renders/ep1.mp4: 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, loudness at -14 LUFS
node tools/render.mjs ep1 --lang en     # with English subtitles
```

Every frame is stepped exactly, so nothing is dropped. The soundtrack is made offline: voices, sound effects and
ambience. It needs Chrome and ffmpeg, and takes about 50 minutes for a 7½-minute episode.

## How it is made

| File | What it holds |
|------|---------------|
| `js/brick/minifig.js` | the minifigure: legs, hips, a printed torso, bending arms, C-hands, a printed face |
| `js/cast/*.js` | a character: its look (`spec`), its build (`build(scene)`), its rest (`idle`, `idleTick`); listed in `js/cast/index.js` |
| `js/moves/*.js` | a move: key poses and their easing, events at moments, a per-frame `tick`, the face, a camera; listed in `js/moves/index.js` |
| `js/fx.js` | the effects: the crescent of sword qi, the blade's trail, sparks, wind, rings, the cut in the floor |
| `js/targets.js` | the post and the wall, every brick its own piece so a cut can split it |
| `js/engine.js` | the world: fighters with marks, walking, turning, gestures, moves, blows, the camera |
| `js/physics.js` | blows that meet (cancel or break through), bodies that can't overlap, the loose ball |
| `js/react.js`, `js/gestures.js` | taking a blow; small actions for films (point, raise, cower, cheer...) |
| `js/films/*.js`, `js/film.js` | an episode, scene by scene (set, hour, who stands where, lines, cues, shots), and its player |
| `js/sets.js`, `js/props.js` | the Longmen Manor and the teahouse; the token, the jade |
| `tools/lines.mjs`, `tools/voices.py` | record an episode's lines in the characters' voices; a line that runs long pushes the rest of its scene back |
| `js/stage.js` | the practice ground at dusk |
| `js/sound.js` | the move's synthesised sounds |

To add a character, write one file in `js/cast/`. To add a move, write one file in `js/moves/`. Each character and
move is self-contained and driven by time, so they can be cast in the films to come: pose a character with a move at
any moment `t`, and its effects follow.
